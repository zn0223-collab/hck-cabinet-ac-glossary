# Cabinet Air Conditioner Glossary

Working definitions for sealed enclosure / cabinet cooling.

## Terms
- **IP55** (IEC 60529): dust-protected and protected against low-pressure water jets. The usual rating for outdoor cabinet air conditioners. See [HCK IP55 cabinet AC](https://www.zjhcc.com/en/products.html).
- **Closed-loop cooling**: recirculates internal air through a refrigerant loop with no exchange of outside air, preserving cabin purity.
- **Rotary compressor**: a compact, efficient compressor type used in HCK units.
- **R410A**: a chlorine-free refrigerant with stable performance across −40 … +55 °C.
- **Cooling load** `Q = Q_internal + U·A·ΔT`: internal equipment heat plus envelope transmission.
- **ΔT**: the temperature difference between the internal setpoint and the ambient.
- **RS485 / Modbus**: the serial monitoring bus used for temperature, alarm and run status.
- **N+1 redundancy**: a standby unit configured for critical loads.
- **CE marking**: HCK units are CE-marked (declaration of conformity).
